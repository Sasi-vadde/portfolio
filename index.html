About

I work where electrochemistry meets system simulation. I combine published correlations into one consistent model, test each new equation on its own in Python against a steady-state case, and only then add it to the Dymola model. Validation against measured data comes last, and when it exposes a defect I trace the cause, such as a density correlation that used the wrong composition variable in place of the KOH mole fraction.

Experience

DLR (German Aerospace Center), Institute of Engineering Thermodynamics · Stuttgart

Intern, Working Student, Master's Thesis, Research Associate (Wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter)

09/2023 – 12/2025

  • Modelling: to give DLR a digital twin of an AWE cell, I built a transient 1D single-cell model in Dymola/Modelica that couples electrochemical, two-phase and thermal equations from published sources, then extended it into a high-pressure stack model.
  • Validation: to cover the low-load regime, where reduced gas dilution is the main safety risk, I validated the gas-purity model against test-rig data at three electrolyte flow rates in mixed and separated circulation. Deviation from measured H2-in-O2 impurity stayed under 5%.
  • Gas crossover: to improve solubility predictions under pressure, I added a fugacity correction for non-ideal gas behaviour and validated the de Groot supersaturation correlation against literature and DLR test-bench data across 1–20 bar and 60–80 °C.
  • Integration: for plant-level KPI assessment, I built a hybrid gas-crossover model (empirical correlations plus data-driven corrections) and integrated it into TEMPEST, DLR's in-house system-level electrochemical simulation platform.
  • Power electronics: to replace a single fixed-efficiency assumption, I rebuilt the converter loss model with literature-based peak-efficiency correlations for MOSFET (97.1%) and GaNFET (98.8%) converters, for more realistic system-level efficiency predictions.
  • Control: for operation under fluctuating renewable load, I developed multi-loop PID control strategies that keep gas purity within safety limits, driven by synthetic ARMA-based wind and PV load profiles I generated in Python.
  • Thermal: to calibrate the Nusselt-number correlations in the thermal stack model, I ran conjugate-heat-transfer CFD in OpenFOAM (chtMultiRegionFoam) of the endplate cooling-channel flow.
  • Test benches: contributed to the development and commissioning of two AWE test benches (50 bar and 5 bar), including P&ID, instrumentation and safety checks, and supported the measurements and data analysis used for model calibration.

Exergoeconomic Optimisation of a 210 MW ISCC Power Plant

To lower the cost of electricity from an integrated solar combined-cycle plant, I applied the Total Revenue Requirement method and optimised five decision variables in iterative runs. Natural gas use fell by 6.2% and the exergy-based levelised cost of electricity dropped from 93.41 to 90.07 €/MWh.

PINN

Applied machine learning · since 02/2026

Physics-Informed Neural Network for Battery State-of-Health

To predict ageing of large-format LFP cells that the model has not seen in training, I am building a PINN in PyTorch that couples a shared MLP with learnable cell embeddings to a Single-Particle-Model ageing model. It predicts capacity zero-shot on held-out cells and forecasts the rest of a cell's trajectory from 70% of its history, evaluated with RMSE, R² and MAPE.

  • PyTorch
  • PINN
  • Single-Particle Model
